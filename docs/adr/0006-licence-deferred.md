# 6. Licence: GNU GPL v3 or later

Status: accepted.

## Decision

Libreri is free software under the **GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later version** (`GPL-3.0-or-later`).

- The full text is in `LICENSE`; every crate and package names the licence (`license = "GPL-3.0-or-later"`), and installers carry it (Linux packages include the file).
- **Why GPL v3:** anyone may use, study, change and share Libreri, and changed versions that are shared must stay free under the same terms. AGPL v3 was considered; its network clause adds little for a desktop app, and GPL v3 is more widely accepted.
- **Compatibility:** every library compiled into Libreri is under a GPL-compatible licence (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC, Zlib, MPL-2.0, Unicode, CC0, OFL for fonts); `docs/third-party-licenses.md` lists them all and is generated by `node scripts/third-party.mjs`. elkjs (Eclipse Public License 2.0, used by Mermaid only for `layout: elk` diagrams) is not GPL-compatible and is left out of the build (`src/lib/stubs/elk.ts`).
- **Separate programs and downloads** (DjVuLibre, Tesseract, unar, eSpeak NG; voices and models) run apart from Libreri and keep their own licences.
- **Looking ahead:** Apple's App Store terms are widely considered incompatible with the GPL; as the copyright holder can publish there regardless, outside contributions should come with a contributor agreement that keeps this possible. Plugins loaded into Libreri would need GPL-compatible licences unless a plugin exception is added.

The privacy policy (`PRIVACY.md`), terms of use (`TERMS.md`) and contributor agreement (`CLA.md`) sit beside the licence.
